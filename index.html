ABOUT MYLIBRARY

Your library, made easier to explore.

MyLibrary is a proposed digital library and book-exchange platform. It aims to help readers discover books, check availability and connect with library services. The first version is a front-end demonstration; live library services will be added after coordination and approval from participating libraries.
